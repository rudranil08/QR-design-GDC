# Brag Plan: QR Designer Launch Video (Startup Edition)

## What is this app?
QR Designer is a fast, 100% browser-only QR code creator and customizer that generates real-time, scannable QR codes for URLs, text, Wi-Fi, email, and phone numbers with crisp SVG/PNG exports and zero tracking.

## The angle
A modern, polished product launch video showing the real app UI in action — highlighting speed, real-time matrix generation, Wi-Fi presets, theme switching, and instant vector export with zero subscriptions.

## Hook (first 3.5 seconds)
"CREATE. CUSTOMIZE. SCAN."
Fast, bold typography on the beat, transitioning directly into the high-precision studio interface.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Live Keystroke Generation: URL typed and matrix immediately calculated with zero latency.
- Format Diversity: Switch to Wi-Fi network setup with instant SSID/password encoding.
- 1-Click Palette Switching: Smooth transition between Classic, Deep Pacific, Warm Sunset, and Midnight OLED.
- High-Resolution Export: Vector SVG and PNG download with WCAG contrast verification.
